Buried Evidence — Requirements
==============================

Player/runtime requirements
---------------------------

- The supplied camera_sd.img artifact.
- Sleuth Kit: mmls, fls, and icat.
- ExifTool for JPEG metadata and embedded-thumbnail extraction.
- 7-Zip for testing and extracting the recovered archive.
- Standard shell tools such as awk, grep, file, strings, and cmp.
- No root privileges or writable loop mount are required for the intended solve.

Author/generator requirements
-----------------------------

- Python 3.
- ImageMagick, providing convert and identify.
- ExifTool.
- 7-Zip.
- Sleuth Kit is useful for verification but is not required by the generator.

The generator creates a 128 MiB raw MBR/FAT32 image without mounting it. The
two evidence files are allocated, then their directory entries are marked
deleted and their FAT chains are released, leaving their data recoverable.
